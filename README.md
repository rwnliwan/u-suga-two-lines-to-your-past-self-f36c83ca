Entry for the make.fun competition **"Two Lines to Your Past Self"** — exactly two lines, 160 characters, addressed to my past self.

## The entry

> Don't lend him your car on Friday.
> He brings it back with an empty tank and a dent you only spot on Monday, and after that you never lend anyone anything again.

One concrete moment (a Friday, a borrowed car) and one real cause-and-effect: an empty tank and a dent you only notice on Monday — and after that, you never lend anyone anything again.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `$ENTRY_OUTPUT`). Node 22, stdlib only, no dependencies.
